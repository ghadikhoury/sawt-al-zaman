# The opening seconds, and respect for the archive

## 1. The hook (first 1–3 seconds)
Most of the brand's views come from people who don't follow the account (Arabic ~81 %, English ~90 %): the first
seconds decide whether a stranger stays.
- **Open a loop** in the first second: state that something happened or exists and hold back the answer («لحنٌ واحد،
  في سهرةٍ واحدة... غيّر حياته»: which melody? which evening?). The brain resists an unresolved question.
- **Show something immediately:** the first frame already has the artist's face or a striking image (no title card),
  and something moves or answers the voice within half a second.
- **Pay it off honestly and in time:** the answer arrives within the film (a payoff that never comes, or comes too
  late, feels manipulative); the curiosity must be grounded in what the film really delivers — not clickbait.
- **What worked on this account before** (brand file): emotional, poetic or surprising lines beat plain factual hooks
  (dates, biographies). A hook can be a paradox («حملت الجزائر في اسمها... وهي بعيدة عنها»), a single decisive moment,
  or a feeling the song is known for.
- Keep it in the brand voice: warm, nostalgic, no shouting, no "you won't believe".
Sources: [Opus: the science of hooks](https://www.opus.pro/blog/the-science-of-hooks),
[Captions: scroll-stopping openings](https://captions.ai/blog/video-hooks-how-to-write-a-scroll-stopping-opening).

## 2. Respect for the archive
- **Don't colourise, upscale with AI, or "restore" faces** of real artists: these overwrite the historical record and
  repaint faces; historians and archivists object to it (FIAF's code separates honest repair of wear from alteration)
  ([Frames: colourisation and the archive](https://framescinemajournal.com/article/colourisation-and-the-archive-repurposing-world-war-one/),
  [PetaPixel: historians on upscaling and colourising](https://petapixel.com/2020/10/05/stop-upscaling-and-colorizing-photos-and-videos-historians-say)).
- Honest repair is fine: deinterlace, denoise lightly, fix levels, add film grain back (`../design/colour-grading.md`).
- A cut-out or a carved/drawn treatment of a photo is a design treatment, clearly so; it must not change the face.
- Captions and dates must be true (keep only what sources agree on); credit every photo.
