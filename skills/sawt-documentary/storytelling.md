# Storytelling for a one-minute Arabic life documentary

Our films tell an artist's life in ~60 s of formal Arabic narration over archive photos and the artist's music. The
owner wants them **nostalgic and cool, not an information documentary** ("not National Geographic"): phrases, photos
and music carry it; facts serve feeling.

## 1. From the Arabic storytelling tradition
- **The ḥakawātī's craft** (the coffee-house storyteller): a known story told so the audience *feels* it again,
  adapted to the listener, the key moment embellished, the room held with rhythm and suspense
  ([Ta'ziyah and Hakawati](https://theatrestyles.blogspot.com/2014/04/taziyah-and-hakawati-arabic-passion.html)).
  Our viewers mostly know these artists: don't explain who Umm Kulthum is; make them feel why she mattered.
- **The frame and the thread (Alf Layla wa Layla):** one framing situation holds many small stories; one story calls
  up the next ([Ouyang, SOAS](https://soas-repository.worktribe.com/output/441817/framing-and-meaning-coincidence-entanglement-and-wonder-in-the-thousand-and-one-nights)).
  For us: an opening line or image the ending returns to (Warda: «حملت الجزائر في اسمها... وهي بعيدة عنها» → the
  finale «وردة الجزائرية»). One governing idea; small scenes on its thread.
- **One thread through the film** (Al Jazeera: «مسار واحد أو خيط»); paragraphs are not separate clips.

## 2. Rhetoric (balāgha) that suits narration — lightly
The script is not a poem (Al Jazeera: not a literary text; avoid poetic delivery). Use these as seasoning:
- **Balance (izdiwāj, muwāzana):** two clauses of near-equal length and weight — «لحنٌ واحد، في سهرةٍ واحدة... غيّر
  حياته». Memorable; proportion "delights the soul" ([al-balagha.com](https://al-balagha.com/wiki/PARALL:_Parallelism)).
- **A light rhymed ending (sajʿ)** at a turn or the close, never throughout ([al-balagha.com: rhymed prose](https://al-balagha.com/wiki/RHYMED:_Rhymed_prose)).
- **Repetition with change (tardīd):** a word returning with a new meaning, e.g. a place named at birth and again at
  the end ([al-balagha.com: repetition](https://al-balagha.com/wiki/REPET:_Repetition)).
- **Short sentences, one image each; concrete nouns over adjectives; no hyperbole** («عبارات التهويل»): tell
  information calmly at its real value.

## 3. Tarab: the emotional engine of this music
Tarab is the ecstatic feeling the music creates; the performer's state is *salṭana*; listeners respond most in the
pause after a cadence (*qafla*) (A. J. Racy, *Making Music in the Arab World*; Frishkopf). For the film:
- **Build, then release:** tension rises through the story, then a pause, then the artist's own voice — our "silence
  before the peak, then her real voice" (the Umm Kulthum film, called perfect).
- **Let the voice land after a breath,** like the qafla: narration stops, a beat of near-silence, then the singing.
- **Repetition is emotional, not redundant** in this tradition: a returning phrase deepens feeling.
Sources: [Aramco World: tarab](https://www.aramcoworld.com/articles/2017/tarab-and-the-art-of-music),
[Afropop: the art of ecstasy](https://afropop.org/audio-programs/tarab-the-art-of-ecstasy-in-arab-music).

## 4. Documentary craft
- **Enrich the picture, don't describe it** («إثراء الصورة وليس الوقوف على وصفها»): add what the image can't say
  ([Al Jazeera Media Institute](https://institute.aljazeera.net/en/node/449)).
- **Don't fill every gap** («أينما وُجد الفراغ وُجد التعليق» is the mistake): let photos and music breathe.
- **Don't burn the ending** («حرق الفيلم»): keep the turn and the legacy for their moment.
- **Large stories through small details; hook immediately; chronology as a compass**; story laws apply (a person, a
  gift, an obstacle, a turn, a resolution) (Ken Burns, [MasterClass](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/ken-burns-shares-tips-for-structuring-a-documentary)).

## 5. The 60-second shape
1. **Hook (0–5 s):** one strong image and one balanced line framing the life (a paradox, or one moment that changed everything).
2. **Origin:** place and year, one small concrete detail.
3. **The gift or the struggle:** what made them; one obstacle.
4. **The turn:** the moment the world heard them (a person, an evening, a song).
5. **The peak:** the great songs or moment.
6. **Legacy and name:** what remains; the name lands big; return to the opening frame.
7. **The real voice** after a breath; then the logo, silent.
About 11 short lines of narration; the artist's face on screen at least every 15 s. The artist's vibe colours the tone
lightly (Fairuz gentle and romantic; Abdel Halim young and genuine, dark only at the illness; Wadih strong and caring).
