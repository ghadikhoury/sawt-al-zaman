## Core rules
1. **Sound marks attention.** A short effect only on what the eye should follow; most motion is silent.
2. **The singing is sacred:** no effects over foreground singing; no sound on the logo; no echo tail.
3. **Short and purposeful:** no beds or hums under the film; every effect brief.
4. **Clean music, no audible edits:** find and hide every seek in his recordings; choose sections deliberately.
5. **Everything glides:** ducks, level changes and stem swaps are smoothed; music a notch lower than instinct.
6. **−14 LUFS, true peak −1.5 dB**, checked on the final file.
7. **Narration:** Charon, temperature 0, his prompt, full diacritics, the whole script in one take, Whisper check.
8. **Never spend money or credits** on voice generation; use the free AI Studio playground in his Chrome.
