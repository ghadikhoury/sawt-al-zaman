# Transitions: a catalogue, and how to choose

Most cuts should be **hard cuts or invisible transitions**; accent transitions stay rare so they feel special.

## 1. Invisible (seamless) transitions — the premium default
| Transition | How | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Match cut** (graphic / shape / motion) | two shots share a shape, a framing or a movement: a round lamp becomes the moon, a ridge becomes a waveform | to link two places or eras by an idea |
| **Carry-over element** (liked) | one element holds while the world changes around it | moving through time with one constant (a photo, a word, a line) |
| **Small detail becomes the next hero** (liked) | a minor element grows into the next scene's subject | zooming the story into a detail |
| **Occlusion / mask** | something passes in front of the camera (a shape, a dark edge, a person, a word) and hides the cut | moving between photos without a visible cut |
| **Whip pan** (liked in Wadih) | fast camera move with motion blur in both shots, same direction | energy, a change of place |
| **Sliding push** (liked) | the next shot pushes the old one, strips slide, motion blur, no colour fringes | in a run of photos |
| **J-cut / L-cut** | sound changes before or after the picture | always available; hides any cut |
| **Window swell-open** (liked) | a window word grows until its inside becomes the next shot | from a title into footage |
| **Aspect-ratio opening** (liked) | a narrow frame opens to a wider one, images moving | a turn in the story or the music |
| **Ink / watercolour bloom, water drop into a wash** (liked, Fairuz) | a wash spreads and becomes the next image | gentle, romantic artists |
Sources: [Filmdaft: 16 transition types](https://filmdaft.com/?p=24604), [Insta360: film transitions](https://www.insta360.com/blog/tips/how-to-create-film-transitions.html).

## 2. Accent transitions (a few per film at most)
Fast push-in zoom with a little radial blur (~0.15 s) into a tighter framing; whip-blur (~0.15 s) — both from the
owner's premium reference.
Light leak or soft light flash (blend `screen`, one or two in a film; more looks like a filter), speed ramp into a
cut, a short flurry of photos (3–6 frames each) on a musical build, a cut to near-black before the peak.

## 3. Banned
Glitch / RGB split / VHS bleed, solarised flash, zoom-through exits, spinning or 3-D cube transitions, wipes with
patterns, the same transition between every shot.

## 4. Rhythm across cuts (montage, after Eisenstein)
Use the classical montage types as tools ([PremiumBeat: Soviet montage](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/soviet-montage-theory-types)):
- **Metric:** shots of a fixed length (a steady run of photos on the beat) — for a list or the passing of years.
- **Rhythmic:** length follows the movement inside the shot — cut when the motion completes.
- **Tonal:** cuts follow the emotional tone (light/dark, calm/tense) — the illness turning dark and back.
- **Overtonal:** all of the above combined — the climax.
- **Intellectual:** two images side by side make a third meaning (a stage photo, then an empty chair).
Pick the transition by what the story needs at that cut (Murch: emotion first), then check it isn't the same as the
last two.
