# Sawt Al-Zaman design

The brand: old classical Arabic music, **nostalgic, warm, premium, mature**. Not a kids' show, not "AI slop", not an
information documentary ("not National Geographic"). Owner's references for "premium": seamless, minimalist, Apple-like.

## The rules that always apply
1. **One idea per film, one hero per moment.** Write the film's idea in one sentence before designing. Each frame has
   one focus; everything else is quieter or waits (staging, Hick's law).
2. **Depth, not flat layers.** The subject is cut out; text and shapes sit between layers; parallax and soft focus
   give space. Things never look pasted on top.
3. **Empty space is part of the design.** At least a third of a frame stays quiet. Don't fill gaps.
4. **Brand colours for all text**: sand, parchment, champagne (muted), terracotta, dusty rose, deep brown; brick red
   occasionally. No blue, green or maroon text. One accent colour per frame. Backgrounds free within the warm range.
5. **Real things, real proportions.** Real photos (artist's face at least every 15 s in documentaries), real flags and
   symbols, true aspect ratios, never stretched; no crop through a face.
6. **Professional, not cartoon.** No clip-art props (candles, flowers, stick figures, simple vector objects). Prefer
   real photos with depth and camera moves, fine line work, masks, texture, light. Cartoonish only when it truly fits.
7. **Reflect the artist lightly.** The artist's vibe is a flavour, never one theme for the whole film (rejected:
   darkroom, paper theatre, "Roman Empire" columns). Each film gets a new style; scenes change.
8. **Bright and legible on a phone.** Gloom only as a story moment. Check every still at phone size.
9. **Restraint.** Few devices per film, each with a story reason. Glitch, RGB split, colour fringes, neon-poster glow
   and flashy zoom-through exits are banned.

## Artist vibes the owner named
Fairuz: gentle, womanly, romantic, morning sometimes. Abdel Halim: gentleman, young, genuine, sings of happiness (dark
only around his illness). Wadih el Safi: Lebanese, manly, strong, caring. Umm Kulthum's film is the bar
("magnificent, literally perfect"): one governing idea, real archive photos, a rich drawn world, beats on the narration
words, each device used once with a reason.

## Process
1. Idea sentence → 2. key frames as **stills first** (pose to pose) → 3. check each still against principles.md and
the layout self-check → 4. only then animate → 5. contact sheet of the render (every ~2.5 s) and fix before showing.
When the brief is open, show two or three clearly different directions as stills; the owner often can't describe what
he pictures but knows it when he sees it.
