# What "premium" means to the owner (confirmed 2026-11-18)

From three reference reels he chose (`../motion-edit/references/premium_*.mp4`) and his comments. He said our older
work "feels like a kids should be watching this"; the references feel "like Apple's design: seamless, minimalist".

## Confirmed (his words: "exactly", "perfect", "100 % correct")
1. **Things live inside the scene, not pasted on top.** Words and shapes pass behind the subject's head, orbit around
   the subject with the back half hidden, a ribbon wraps behind then in front. Needs the subject cut out.
2. **Every move has a reason and good timing.** Words arrive from outside the frame, physically: ease-out (fast
   arrival, soft landing). No plain fades, no even-speed slides.
3. **Contrast in type within a narrow palette.** Heavy beside light (one bold + one thin font), big beside small,
   few fonts and a narrow colour range. → `../text/SKILL.md`.
4. **Pace with sound tied to attention.** A short sound marks only what the viewer should look at. → `../sound-voice`.
5. **Frame (aspect-ratio) change only as a transition**: a narrow frame opening to wider while the images keep moving.
   Constant frame changing "doesn't look neat".
Also liked: rapid photo changes with a sound per change; a big background photo with a smaller main photo on top,
each changing on its own rhythm; the fast photo pop-up collage; a cut-out artist with text behind them.

## Agreed but "obvious" (baseline, not a feature)
Light instead of decoration (faint warm glow), one consistent grade, soft falloff, fine grain.

## From the references he does **not** want
Text stuck flat on a wall; loud glowing letters; big white capitals; reel 3's big fonts; glitch and colour fringes.

## How to get there (our translation into practice)
- Fewer elements, more empty space, one hero per moment (layout.md §1–2).
- Depth: cut-out subject, text between layers, parallax, soft focus behind (layout.md §5).
- Motion with physics and purpose: anticipation, ease-out arrival, follow-through, then hold (principles.md).
- Typography with contrast, not decoration (text skill).
- Sound only on attention points (sound-voice skill).
- Before/after test frames: `before_after/before/` holds seven of our old frames (lyric over footage, name card, map,
  drawn graphic, photo collage, portrait + title, name finale) to be redone in this style side by side.
